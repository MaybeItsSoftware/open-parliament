Every word said in Parliament, in your pocket. Free, with no ads, and open source.

Open Parliament turns the official record of the House of Commons and House of Lords into a fast, readable app. Read debates exactly as they were spoken, see who said what, check how your MP voted, and follow new laws from first reading to Royal Assent.

READ THE DEBATES
• Every Commons, Lords, Westminster Hall and Grand Committee sitting, word for word from Hansard
• Each contribution shows the speaker's photo, party and role, even when Hansard only calls them "The Minister"
• Votes appear as clear results: how many voted each way, and who
• Watch the video of a debate on parliamentlive.tv, or jump straight to the moment a speech began
• Browse by day on a sitting calendar that marks recesses

FOLLOW YOUR MP
• Profiles for every MP and member of the House of Lords, with their recent contributions and voting record
• Constituency pages with the latest general election result, majority, turnout and vote share
• See the current make-up of each House on a seating chart

TRACK THE LAW
• Bills going through Parliament now, with every stage and upcoming committee sittings
• Read the full text of a bill
• A timeline of Acts of Parliament
• Look up the Standing Orders, the rules that govern the House of Commons

KNOW WHO RUNS YOUR AREA
• A national map coloured by who holds each constituency and council
• Council pages with seat breakdowns, control history going back decades, and every ward councillor

BUILT FOR READING
• One search box for debates, MPs, constituencies, councils, councillors and bills
• Save speeches and share quotes
• Days you have opened stay on your phone, so you can read them offline
• No account and no ads

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Open Parliament is free, has no ads and no subscriptions. The full source code is public under the MIT licence, so anyone can check how it works or help improve it: https://github.com/MaybeItsSoftware/open-parliament

DATA SOURCES
Open Parliament shows public data from official UK Parliament services: Hansard (hansard.parliament.uk), the Members API, the Bills API and parliamentlive.tv. Contains Parliamentary information licensed under the Open Parliament Licence v3.0.

Council control and councillors: OpenCouncilData (CC BY-SA 4.0). Councillor photos and contacts: Democracy Club (CC BY 4.0). Boundaries: Office for National Statistics. Maps: © OpenStreetMap contributors, © CARTO.
